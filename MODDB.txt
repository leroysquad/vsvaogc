Client-only. Required on server: no.

Vintage Story leaks OpenGL MeshRefs. When a VAO is garbage-collected, VAO.Finalize logs "leaking memory, missing call to Dispose" and does not delete the GL buffers. Those buffers can only be deleted on the render thread. Over a long session VRAM fills with dead meshes, FPS slides, and hitching comes back — even on a 3090.

This mod Harmony-patches the client:

1. VAO.Finalize — queue the undisposed VAO instead of leaking it
2. ClientMain.ExecuteMainThreadTasks — dispose a budgeted number of queued VAOs per frame on the GL thread (16 normally, 32 if pending>=64, 64 if pending>=256)
3. On a hitch (>= 80 ms) drain more of the queue on the render thread. No GC.Collect.
4. 1.1.3 writes frameguard.json off the render thread so metrics I/O cannot hitch the frame.

Chat: .vaogc  → version / queued / reclaimed / pending

Does not change your graphics settings. Does not run on the server. Safe for public multiplayer (same bucket as a texture pack).

Known vanilla leak triggers include harvestable skeps in inventory/handbook. This patch covers those plus any other MeshRef the engine forgets to Dispose.

Tested on Vintage Story 1.22.6, Windows, NVIDIA.
